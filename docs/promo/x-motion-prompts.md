## C. Tips people repeat (from the posts above)
- Start from the one-line showreel prompt, then add your product name, 3 features, and your colors.
- Give it real material: your logo, a screenshot, or your blog text. Short prompts work when the AI has real content.
- Ask for a set length (15 or 20 seconds) and a style (Apple showreel).
- Add a skill or template on top ('motionmaxxing', the rossaxbt template) for a more designed look.
- Sound on: the best examples add music and sound effects.
